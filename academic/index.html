MSc Project

Does Artificial Intelligence Enhance Student Engagement?

T803 — MSc Computing Project
The Open University, 2025

A comprehensive research project investigating the impact of artificial intelligence tools on student engagement in higher education. The study examined how AI-powered active learning generates significantly higher engagement compared with traditional passive learning methods, with students in AI-enhanced programmes achieving markedly improved outcomes.

The project included a systematic literature review, analysis of current research metrics, and a SWOT analysis of AI adoption for student engagement. It explored the pedagogical implications of tools such as ChatGPT and adaptive learning platforms within defence and higher education contexts.

Key areas:

  • AI-powered active learning vs. traditional pedagogy
  • Student engagement metrics and measurement frameworks
  • Implementation challenges in military and defence education
  • Ethical considerations and AI governance in educational settings
