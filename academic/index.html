MSc Project

Does Artificial Intelligence Enhance Student Engagement?

T803 — MSc Computing Project · 2025

A comprehensive research project examining the growing body of evidence that artificial intelligence technologies significantly enhance student engagement across diverse educational contexts, while also addressing the substantial implementation challenges that institutions must navigate.

The research demonstrated that AI-powered active learning generates ten times more engagement than traditional passive learning methods (Engageli, 2024), and that students in AI-enhanced programmes achieve 54% higher test scores than those in conventional learning environments. The study found that ChatGPT and similar large language models show a large positive impact on learning performance (effect size = 0.867), with 94.3% student recognition and 90.4% usage rates among higher education students (Frontiers in Education, 2025).

Key areas of investigation included:

  • Generative AI and Large Language Models: ChatGPT's impact on learning perception (0.456) and higher-order thinking (0.457), with particular effectiveness in problem-based learning environments
  • Adaptive Learning Platforms: Arizona State University reported 10% increased retention rates; DreamBox Learning showed 25% improvement in maths proficiency with 60 minutes weekly usage, and 30% increases in overall student engagement
  • Intelligent Tutoring Systems: 25% improvements in grades, test scores, and learning outcomes across controlled experimental studies (Naseer et al., 2024)
  • AI-Powered Assessment: Feedback delivered ten times faster than traditional methods, enabling continuous micro-assessments; 78.2% of students verify AI-provided answers, indicating responsible usage patterns
  • Implementation Challenges: Ethical considerations, AI governance frameworks, digital divide issues, and the particular challenges of adopting AI within defence and military education settings

The project included a systematic literature review, comprehensive analysis of current research metrics, a SWOT analysis of AI adoption for student engagement, and practical recommendations for AI integration within the Ministry of Defence Learning Academy context.
