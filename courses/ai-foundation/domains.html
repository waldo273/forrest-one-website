The Eight Domains

The syllabus is divided into eight knowledge domains, each subdivided into specific areas.

How to read a domain

Each domain page lists the areas the syllabus tests, the learning outcomes a candidate should be able to demonstrate, and the resources available for that section. Areas marked (awareness) in the syllabus are tested only at recognition level.
