What the course covers

The UK AI Foundation (UKAIF) certification is the UK's core qualification for working with artificial intelligence — from how AI systems learn to how to use and govern them responsibly.

About this course

This pathway is delivered by Billy Forrest, a UK AI Council (UKAIF) Certified AI Trainer. Training is practical and scenario-led, mirroring the way AI is actually applied in security and business operations.

By the end you'll be ready to sit the UK AI Foundation certification and use AI confidently and responsibly in your work.
