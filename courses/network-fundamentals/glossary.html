Glossary

216 key terms drawn from all twelve sessions. Each entry shows the session it comes from, so you can jump straight to the context.

How to use this

These are the terms the course expects you to use precisely. CompTIA questions often turn on a distinction between two terms that sound similar — authentication and authorisation, IDS and IPS, hashing and encryption. If two entries look alike, learn the difference.
