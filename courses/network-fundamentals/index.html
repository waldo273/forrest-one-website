What this course is

This is a combined course. Sessions 1–3 teach the network fundamentals that CompTIA Security+ assumes you already know: models and addressing, switching and segmentation, and the network services that carry traffic. Sessions 4–12 then cover the Security+ SY0-701 syllabus proper.

Security+ exam format: maximum of 90 questions · 90 minutes · multiple-choice and performance-based · pass mark 750 on a scale of 100–900.

Why networks first: every security control — a firewall rule, an IDS signature, a VPN tunnel — speaks the language of networks, and every attack travels over them.

The twelve sessions

Each session has a narrated video, a deck, a chapter of the student notes and a quiz. Click a session to watch the walkthrough and get the materials.

Session 01 · Network foundation

Network Models & IP Addressing

The language of networks: the OSI and TCP/IP models, encapsulation and port addressing, the physical and data link layers, media and topologies, then the IPv4 addressing and subnetting that security controls depend on.

Session 02 · Network foundation

Switching, Routing & Network Architecture

The devices and the paths: switches and routers, cloud networking and routing foundations, route selection, NAT and inter-VLAN routing, VLANs and Spanning Tree, and wireless and physical deployment.

Session 03 · Network foundation

Network Services, Operations & Troubleshooting

The operational core: ports and protocols, documentation and agreements, the life-cycle and controlled change, configuration and monitoring tools, baselines and resilience, and the core services that keep networks usable.

Session 04 · Security+ core

Security Controls, Concepts & Zero Trust

Why controls exist and how they are classified: control categories and functional types, non-repudiation and authentication factors, accounting and audit trails, zero trust, deception technology, and controlled change.

Session 05 · Security+ core

Threat Actors, Attack Surfaces & Vectors

Know the adversary: actor types and motivations, the attack surface, the vulnerability taxonomy, malware and credential attacks, network attacks, and the mitigations that close them.

Session 06 · Security+ core

Cryptography, PKI & Identity

The three jobs of cryptography and its vocabulary, integrity and signatures, the certificate and PKI paper trail, identity and access management, and the models that control access.

Session 07 · Security+ core

Security Architecture & Data Protection

Designing for trust: architecture and deployment models, compute and edge platforms, secure design principles, data protection and classification, resilience and recovery.

Session 08 · Security architecture

Network Security Controls & Segmentation

Defending the network: physical and human controls, segmentation, guest and personal devices, hardening and admission control, key and content control, and trust zones.

Session 09 · Security architecture

Network Troubleshooting & Resilience

Diagnosing the network: troubleshooting methodology, cable and interface faults, wireless and switching faults, routing, filtering and addressing problems, congestion and packet loss, and the troubleshooting toolset.

Session 10 · Operations & governance

Vulnerability Management & Asset Protection

From a known good state: baselines and hardening, mobile and wireless, secure coding and monitoring, the asset life-cycle, vulnerability management, and the monitoring tools that prove it.

Session 11 · Operations & governance

Security Operations, Monitoring & Forensics

Running the defence: monitoring and network security devices, endpoint and data controls, identity and automation, incident response, threat hunting and forensic evidence, and log investigation.

Session 12 · Operations & governance

Governance, Risk, Compliance & Awareness

Turning security into behaviour: governance foundations, external obligations and risk identification, risk analysis and treatment, reporting and business impact, compliance, privacy and audit, and assurance and awareness.
