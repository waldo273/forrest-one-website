Practice exam

A full-length mock paper, 90 questions, weighted to the real SY0-701 domain split. Take it under exam conditions: no notes, one sitting, then review every explanation before you retake it.

How this paper is built

The real examination is a maximum of 90 questions in 90 minutes, and CompTIA weights the five domains. This paper matches that weighting exactly, so the proportion of each subject you meet here is the proportion you will meet on the day.

DomainWeight Questions here
1. General Security Concepts12%11
2. Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations22%20

Pass mark: 750 on a scale of 100 to 900, which is commonly taken as roughly 83%. Below that, treat the paper as a diagnostic: the explanations tell you which domain to revisit.
