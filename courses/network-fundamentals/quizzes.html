Quizzes

Twelve interactive quizzes, twenty questions each, with immediate feedback and shuffled answer positions.

How to use these

The interactive quizzes are for learning: every question explains why the answer is right and why each distractor is wrong. Work a paper after the matching session content and again before the exam.
