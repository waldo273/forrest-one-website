Reading strategy

Use the all in one student notes as your first pass: they carry the key terms, the core concepts, the reference deep dives, the cautions and the exam tips for every session. Go to the reference texts when a concept needs more depth than the notes give, and use the standards when you need the precise definition — they are the authority, not the textbooks.

Network Fundamentals + CompTIA Security+ SY0-701. Twelve sessions of three hours, delivered as a combined course: sessions 1–3 build the network foundation, sessions 4–12 cover the Security+ syllabus.
