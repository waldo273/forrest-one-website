Scheme of work

Twelve sessions of three hours each, 36 hours total. Sessions 1–3 are the network foundation; sessions 4–12 cover the CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 syllabus.

Delivery model

Each session runs three hours: approximately 2 hours 25 minutes of tuition, a 10-minute break, then 25 minutes of quiz and review. Every session produces the same deliverables, so the rhythm is identical week to week.

Per-session deliverables

  • Session objectives — learning outcomes and exam context
  • Slide deck — 20–32 themed slides, PDF and PowerPoint
  • Instructor notes — timing-marked delivery guide with activities
  • Instructor script — word-for-word delivery per slide
  • Narrated video — the session walkthrough with chapter navigation
  • Interactive quiz — 20 questions with immediate feedback

Assessment

Each session carries a 20-question interactive quiz for learning. The course finishes with consolidation in Session 12.

Security+ examination

Maximum of 90 questions, 90 minutes, multiple-choice and performance-based. Pass mark is 750 on a scale of 100–900. The exam is closed book and proctored.

Network Fundamentals + CompTIA Security+ SY0-701. Twelve sessions of three hours, delivered as a combined course: sessions 1–3 build the network foundation, sessions 4–12 cover the Security+ syllabus.
