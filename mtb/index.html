MIAS MTB Leader Level 2 • Insured • First Aid Trained

MIAS MTB Leader Level 2

I hold the MIAS Mountain Bike Leader Level 2 qualification — the industry-recognised standard for leading groups on technically demanding off-road terrain, including steep descents, rock gardens, and natural trail features.

The Level 2 Leader award covers:

  • Leading groups on red and black-grade trails
  • Advanced risk assessment and group management
  • Trail-side mechanical repairs and emergency procedures
  • Rider assessment and dynamic session planning
  • Environmental awareness and sustainable trail use
